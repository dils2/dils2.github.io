Subway Avenger Privacy Policy

Effective: November 1, 2026
Operator: Work And Play · devmin0724@gmail.com

Subway Avenger (the "Game", package com.wapl.subwayavenger) has no sign-up or account of its own. The operator does not collect or store on any server information that directly identifies you, such as your name, phone number or email. Game progress is stored on your device by default.

Each provider processes this data under its own privacy policy. The operator does not sell your data.

  • Google (Firebase, AdMob, Play Games, Play Billing): https://policies.google.com/privacy · Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Unity Ads: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

3. Retention and deletion

  • On-device data is deleted when you uninstall the Game.
  • Cloud save data can be deleted for this game in the Google Play Games app settings.
  • Analytics and ad data follow each provider's retention policy. Email us below to request deletion.

4. Your choices

  • Opt out of ad personalization or reset your Advertising ID: device Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland see an ad consent form at launch and can change their choice at any time.
  • You can play without signing in to Play Games (only cloud save is unavailable).

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
